**What I work on**

- **Mantel VPN.** Your account is a 16-digit number. No email, no name, pay with Monero, Bitcoin or Litecoin.
- **RAM-only servers.** Nodes boot a signed OS image with [stboot](https://www.system-transparency.org) and keep nothing on disk. Image hashes are public: [`/node-os/index.json`](https://mantelvpn.com/node-os/index.json).
- **Post-quantum tunnel.** WireGuard with an extra post-quantum key exchange, in beta.
- **Port forwarding included.** TCP and UDP per device, for game servers, self-hosting and torrents.

**Stack**

`Go` · `TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `React` · `Rust / Tauri` · `Kotlin` · `WireGuard` · `nftables` · `Linux`
